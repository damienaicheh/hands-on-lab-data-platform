# Corporate Catering Policy 2026

All corporate events must use approved vendors from the Contoso preferred vendor list.
Dietary requirements: Vegetarian, vegan, halal, and gluten-free options mandatory for events >50 people.
Budget caps: Standard events €50/person, executive events €80/person, gala dinners €120/person.
Alcohol policy: Wine and beer only at daytime events. Spirits allowed at evening galas with prior approval.
Sustainability: Single-use plastics prohibited. Local sourcing preferred. Food waste must be <15%.
