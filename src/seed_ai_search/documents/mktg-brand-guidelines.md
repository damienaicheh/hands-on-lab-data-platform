# Brand Guidelines — Event Communications

Logo: Use Contoso primary logo (blue) on white backgrounds. Minimum size: 24px height.
Colors: Primary #0078D4, Secondary #50E6FF, Accent #FFB900. No gradients on print.
Typography: Segoe UI for digital, Segoe UI Semibold for headlines. Minimum body size: 14px.
Photography: Use authentic employee photos, no stock images. Diverse representation required.
Tone of voice: Professional but approachable. Active voice. Short sentences.
Approval process: All external communications must be reviewed by Brand team (48h SLA).
